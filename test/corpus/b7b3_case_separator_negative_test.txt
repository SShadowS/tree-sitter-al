; B7b-3 Task 6 negatives for case-branch separators (spec 4.2.3). The `negative` cases are ERRORs by
; design (alc rejects them). The former `known gap` cases (deferred-work item 47, alc-valid shapes that
; ERRORed) all parse now and live in item_47_case_empty_group_test.txt. `1: ;;` is a known
; OVER-acceptance unchanged from main (rule 4.2.3: keep today's behaviour). The cases after it are
; invalid in every configuration (alc AL0104/AL0224, most also AL0111) but parse clean under the case-separator
; rules: classified over-acceptances, deferred-work item 44's decided limit (spec 4.2.4); the last two are
; admitted by item 47's sibling empty_statement after a group (spec 4.2.3 as amended). Their expected trees are
; the trees we accept (checked by the 0.27 CLI). Verdicts: tools/alc_probe/cases/b7b3/case/.
