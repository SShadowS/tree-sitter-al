depth counter is decremented. v3.2.0 made PREPROC_CLOSE scanner-exclusive
for that; since state review C1 `#endif` is the grammar regex
`(?i)#[ \t]*endif`, gated by the scanner, and the depth decrement is the
zero-width `_endif_mark` right after it. Pre-3.2.0 a grammar-literal fallback
bypassed the depth counter entirely, a latent bug this test used to
unknowingly document; see preproc_if_elif_whitespace_tolerance_test.txt)
