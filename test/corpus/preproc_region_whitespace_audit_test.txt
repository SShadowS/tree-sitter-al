AUDIT FIX — NEGATIVE: `#` then a bare LF then `region` on the next line must
NOT be recognized as `#region`. `preproc_region`/`preproc_endregion` used
`#\s*region`/`#\s*endregion` (regex `\s` matches `\n`/`\r`, same cross-line
hazard class as the `#pragma` defect) — confirmed pre-fix this silently
parsed as ONE `preproc_region` token spanning both lines, swallowing real
source. Tightened to `[ \t]*` (horizontal-only) alongside the pragma fix. The
CRLF variant (`#\r\nregion`) shares this exact proof and was verified
empirically via a standalone (uncommitted) `tree-sitter parse` scratch
