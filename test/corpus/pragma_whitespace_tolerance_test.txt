================================================================================
NEGATIVE: `#` then a bare LF then `pragma` on the next line must NOT be
recognized as a pragma directive — horizontal-only tolerance (`[ \t]*`), never
`\s*` (which would swallow the newline and the next line's real content). The
CRLF variant (`#\r\npragma`) shares this exact proof — `[ \t]*` excludes `\r`
by construction the same way it excludes `\n` — and was verified empirically
via a standalone (uncommitted) `tree-sitter parse` scratch fixture rather than
here: this repo's `.gitattributes` forces `eol=lf` on every committed text
file, so a `\r\n` byte sequence in a committed corpus fixture is silently
normalized back to `\n` on checkout, making a committed "CRLF" test
indistinguishable from this LF one — not honest coverage, hence not added.
================================================================================
