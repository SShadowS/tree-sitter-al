================================================================================
An expression continued across a #if boundary stays inside its assignment
--
alc 18.0.37.11445 compiles this. Before the fix the parser produced
`assignment_statement` with `right: (integer)` -- just the 1 -- and then
`+ 2` and `+ 3` as floating UNFIELDED children of the statement run. The
program is `1 + 3` or `1 + 2 + 3`; the tree was neither. Zero ERROR nodes,
every byte covered, every node type ordinary: what moved was the PARENT,
which is the one thing no byte-coverage or node-count check can see.
.
0 occurrences in BC.History and in DC, so no corpus gate would ever have
caught it. Found by an adversarial review of tools/validate_al_file.py,
whose `orphan-operator-expr` check now reports the shape if it returns.
.
The fix is that `preproc_conditional_expression_tail` absorbs the
continuations that FOLLOW `#endif` itself. Leaving them to a repeat in the
host rule loses them to the statement alternative -- measured, four times.
================================================================================

codeunit 50109 PreprocExpr
{
    procedure P() r: Integer
    begin
        r := 1
#if CLEAN25
            + 2
#endif
            + 3;
    end;
}
